CaseSimpli — Making legal research simpler and more efficient

The Founder Origin: I founded CaseSimpli as a 400-level law student after experiencing first-hand how time-consuming, stressful and cumbersome legal research could be. After discovering that the problem extended beyond my own experience, I built CaseSimpli to make legal materials easier to access and understand, while using AI to make the research process more efficient.

Full Case Study • Problem, Validation, Product Architecture & Decisions
01

The Problem

During my fourth year in law school (400-level), I experienced the relentless friction involved in conducting legal research. Finding relevant judicial precedents meant sifting through hundreds of pages of unindexed law reports, dense judgments, and archaic language under strict academic and litigation deadlines.

Observing fellow law students and colleagues, I realized they shared the exact same struggle: hours lost searching for the locus classicus, difficulty isolating the specific ratio decidendi, and constant fatigue from cumbersome repositories.

casesimpli.com/about
2. Core Proposition — "Legal Research at its simplest & most comprehensible form." 02 / 05
02

From Personal Problem to Product Opportunity

Instead of assuming my personal frustration was unique, I investigated whether the friction was widespread across faculties and institutions. Through targeted outreach, we partnered with law student bodies across more than 10 universities in Nigeria, validating the acute demand for accessible legal research and creating a trusted student-led distribution network.

The Discovery & Validation Arc: Personal research friction → investigation among peers → partnerships with student bodies in 10+ universities → building a structured, simplified legal repository.
03

The Product

CaseSimpli was designed to make legal materials direct, readable, and structured. Key product capabilities included:

  • Structured Case Summaries: Clean breakdowns isolating Facts of the Case, Legal Issues, and Decision of the Court.
  • 12 Curated Legal Domains: Categorized navigation across Supreme Court, Court of Appeal, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, Human Rights, Evidence, Contract, Torts, Commercial Law, and Company Law.
  • On-Demand Case Requests: Direct user request flow allowing researchers to ask for specific unindexed judicial precedents.
  • CaseSimpli AI Assistant: Conversational legal assistant capable of clarifying statutory distinctions and pointing to relevant statutory provisions.
casesimpli.com/categories
3. Domain Taxonomy & Architecture — High-level feature cards and 12 structured legal categories for intuitive browsing. 03 / 05
04

Why AI?

AI was introduced strictly as a capability to reduce friction within the research workflow—never as a gimmick. The goal was to help users query complex legal principles in natural language (e.g., distinguishing between murder and manslaughter provisions under the Criminal Code) and receive structured, digestible syntheses instantly.

AI did not attempt to replace legal counsel or fabricate judicial authority; instead, it acted as an intelligent retrieval and synthesis layer guiding researchers to relevant statutory sections and doctrines.

PRODUCT 02 Legal Workflows • Document Automation • Business Productivity

Clauza — Simplifying legal work for businesses and startups

Clauza is a legal workflow platform designed to help businesses and startups create, review, collaborate on, and manage legal documents more efficiently. It combines structured workflows, automation, and AI to reduce friction across recurring legal tasks.

End-to-End
Workflow Integration

From structured drafting and clause configuration to multi-party collaboration and digital execution.

Workflow Discovery Document Generation Multi-Party Collaboration E-Signature Lifecycle

The Product Challenge: "How can recurring legal work become simpler and more accessible for businesses and startups?" Clauza explores how structured workflows, automation, and AI can reduce friction across drafting, review, collaboration, and execution.

clauza.app/platform
1. Clauza Business Platform — Democratizing access to legal services for growing businesses and startups. 01 / 04
Full Case Study • Workflow Discovery, Product Design & Commercial Learning
01

The Problem

Founders, operators, and small businesses regularly manage recurring legal documents—commercial leases, non-disclosure agreements, contractor terms, and service agreements. For organizations without dedicated in-house legal teams, recurring legal work is often:

  • Fragmented & Disconnected: Teams bounce between generic templates, word processors, email threads, PDF markups, and separate e-signature tools.
  • Slow & Manual: Adapting standard clauses to specific commercial terms requires manual copy-pasting, increasing the risk of omitted or inconsistent provisions.
  • Costly for Routine Tasks: Engaging external law firms for standard agreements is expensive and introduces operational delays for early-stage teams.
02

The Product Opportunity

The product opportunity was to turn disjointed legal tasks into a cohesive, manageable digital workflow. Instead of treating drafting, negotiation, and signing as isolated events, Clauza was conceived to help businesses:

  • Generate legally sound agreements using guided, structured inputs rather than unverified generic templates.
  • Collaborate directly with counterparties in a shared workspace to finalize commercial details.
  • Execute and store documents in one unified lifecycle, removing third-party tool hopping.
clauza.app/assistant
2. Multi-Device Legal Assistant — Contextual legal assistance, contract review support, dictation, and document history across devices. 02 / 04
03

Designing the Workflow

Clauza translated complex legal procedures into structured digital workflows designed around practical user needs:

  • Guided Document Generation: Structured form wizards capture essential business terms (parties, rent amounts, covenants, term lengths) and automatically compile customized legal agreements.
  • Multi-Party Collaboration: Integrated invitation flows allow counterparties to review drafts, complete required information, and resolve details without messy email threads.
  • Integrated E-Signature & Lifecycle: In-app digital signature capture and version tracking to finalize executed agreements in a single continuous process.
  • Contextual Legal Assistant: Voice and text assistant supporting document drafting, explaining unfamiliar clauses, and testing legal queries.
clauza.app/collaborate
3. Multi-Party Collaboration Flow — Invite Collaborator modal enabling shared drafting and party detail completion. 03 / 04
clauza.app/document-editor
4. Document Lifecycle & E-Signature — Step-by-step form execution, signature capture, and active draft management. 04 / 04
04

Where Technology Adds Value

Rather than viewing AI as the starting premise, Clauza followed a disciplined product logic:

1. Identify the Friction
Dense clause formulation, terminology ambiguity, and disjointed revision cycles across disconnected tools.
2. Design the Workflow
Structured step-by-step inputs and guided form wizards that give structure to document creation before introducing technology.
3. Apply Technology Where Useful
Generative drafting suggestions, plain-language clause explanations, and automated consistency checks to accelerate routine work.
4. Preserve User Control
Explicit human-in-the-loop approval controls where users review, edit, and approve every clause, ensuring users remain responsible for important legal decisions.
05

06

Commercial & Product-Market Learning

Building Clauza provided critical firsthand lessons in B2B product adoption and commercial viability. Solving a real workflow problem does not automatically guarantee willingness to pay:

  • Problem Frequency vs. Willingness to Pay: B2B software adoption depends heavily on how often the user encounters the problem. For early-stage businesses, routine legal agreements occur periodically rather than daily, making standalone recurring subscriptions harder to justify.
  • Perceived Value & Ad-Hoc Workarounds: Users frequently recognize friction in their workflow but may tolerate manual workarounds unless the product demonstrates undeniable recurring ROI.
  • Understanding Adoption Barriers: Effective product management requires looking beyond user pain points to investigate purchasing behavior, budget ownership, and switching friction.
•

Key Product Takeaway

"Building Clauza reinforced that solving a real workflow problem is only part of product development. The experience also highlighted the challenge of demonstrating enough recurring value for businesses to justify paying for legal technology. It reinforced the importance of understanding not only user pain points, but also purchasing behavior, adoption barriers, perceived value, and the business case behind a product."

PRODUCT 03 Contract Review • Deal Protection • Negotiation

PactIQ — Understand your contract. Protect what matters. Get a better deal.

PactIQ helps freelancers, creators, influencers, and independent professionals understand the contracts behind their opportunities, identify terms that could affect them, protect what matters, and prepare for negotiation before they sign.

Review → Action
Contract-to-Deal Workflow

From understanding the agreement and identifying important terms to preparing negotiation responses and tracking revisions.

Contract Understanding Risk Identification Deal Protection Negotiation Assistance

The Product Challenge: "How can people make better decisions about contracts before they sign?" PactIQ uses AI to help users understand their agreements, identify important terms and risks, protect their interests, and take action during negotiation.

pactiq.ai/platform
1. Contract Intelligence Platform — Plain-English contract analysis, risk detection, and negotiation strategy for independent professionals. 01 / 06
Full Case Study • Problem Discovery, Actionable UX Architecture & Product Leadership
01

The Problem

Freelancers, creators, influencers, and independent professionals regularly encounter contracts as part of securing work, partnerships, campaigns, and other opportunities.

Receiving a contract does not necessarily mean understanding the full commitment. Users may focus on headline terms such as payment or deliverables while overlooking provisions around usage rights, exclusivity, intellectual property, liability, payment timing, termination, or other obligations.

Legal review can provide deeper protection, but seeking legal advice for every agreement may not always be practical or affordable. This created an opportunity to help users understand what they were agreeing to before making a decision.

Core Product Insight: The moment someone receives a contract is a decision-making moment. PactIQ was designed to give users the clarity and tools they need to evaluate the deal before committing.
pactiq.ai/workspace
2. Role-Tuned Workspace Dashboard — Deals repository with priority-tuned risk checks (e.g. Creator Workspace tuned for fair payment & IP protection). 02 / 06
02

The Product Opportunity

PactIQ was not designed merely as a contract summarizer. The product opportunity is to move users from receiving a contract through a structured decision journey:

01. Review
The user uploads the contract (PDF or Word .docx) into their secure workspace for structured analysis.
02. Understand
Important provisions, payment structures, and legal commitments are translated into clear, plain language.
03. Identify What Matters
The product surfaces high-attention terms, ambiguous commitments, and missing protective clauses based on the user's role.
04. Protect
Highlights where the agreement may disadvantage or expose the user, identifying specific terms worth questioning or pushing back on.
05. Negotiate
Converts identified issues into practical negotiation points, first-person message drafts, and targeted clause redrafts.
06. Decide
The user remains responsible for the final decision, equipped with the context and language needed to accept, question, or negotiate before signing.
pactiq.ai/deals/contract-intelligence-report
3. Contract Intelligence Report — Categorized findings (High Attention, Worth Reviewing, Missing Protections) and key commercial parameters backed by verified contract text. 03 / 06
03

From Review to Negotiation

Identifying a risky clause is only useful if the user understands why it matters and what they can do about it. PactIQ therefore turns contract findings into actionable next steps, moving through: Issue → Explanation → Action → Negotiation.

  • Personalized Negotiation Messaging: Converts identified issues into clear, first-person negotiation messages that users can review, adapt, and send to the other party.
  • Targeted Clause Redrafting: Generates alternative wording for selected clauses while keeping the requested change focused on the specific issue.
  • Version Comparison & Deal Progression: Compares revised versions of an agreement to show which negotiation points were addressed, partially addressed, or remain unresolved.
pactiq.ai/deals/version-comparison
4. Deal Progression & Version Tracking — Tracking status of previous concerns across negotiation rounds (Addressed vs Unresolved). 04 / 06
pactiq.ai/deals/execute-strategy
5. Contract Strategy Hub — Direct execution flows for 1st-person email generation, version comparison, and targeted redrafts. 05 / 06
04

AI Product Thinking

PactIQ was designed around a simple principle: AI should help users move from understanding a contract to taking informed action. Rather than making the product primarily conversational, AI is embedded at specific points in the workflow where it can provide useful analysis, explanation, and drafting support:

Review-First, Not Chatbot-First
Users often do not know what they should be asking about a contract. Starting with an empty chat places too much responsibility on the user. PactIQ instead begins with an automated review that surfaces important issues and gives users a useful starting point for deeper exploration.
Source-Grounded Analysis
Findings and risk alerts are linked to relevant provisions in the uploaded agreement, allowing users to trace the analysis back to the underlying contract text.
Role-Aware Analysis
Different users need to protect different things. PactIQ uses the user's role and priorities (e.g. creator vs freelancer) to make its analysis more relevant to the context of the agreement.
AI-Assisted Action
AI does not stop at identifying problems. It helps turn findings into negotiation messages and targeted clause revisions that users can review and adapt.
User Control & Agency
PactIQ supports the user's decision-making rather than making the decision for them. Important actions and final determinations remain under the user's control.
